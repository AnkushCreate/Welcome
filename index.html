Selected builds

Wearable tentacle arm

ESP32, 5 servos, 3D-printed PLA+

A Doc Ock-style cable-driven arm, 100 cm long, worn on the body. Printed segments ride on a 6 mm nylon rod spine, four servos pull the tendons and a fifth drives the claw. It is controlled from a web interface served by the ESP32.

Quadruped spider robot

ESP32, ESP-CAM, PCA9685, ESP-NOW

A four-legged walker on MG996R servos with animatronic eyes and obstacle avoidance. Wireless control runs over ESP-NOW, and the camera board handles vision.

Electromagnetic maglev train

PID control, electromagnets

A DIY levitating train held in place by a PID loop that keeps the gap steady.

Ornithopter and gesture-controlled servos

ESP32, PCA9685, servos

A flapping-wing aircraft, plus a gesture-driven servo system with a web UI for controlling the motion.

GPS tracker

NEO-6M, NodeMCU ESP8266

A tracker that creates its own WiFi hotspot and serves a live location dashboard. Work in progress.
